ABOUT US

Welcome to the Ebubechukwu family

We are a growing community of special educators, therapists, parents and volunteers in Abuja. Together we make sure no child is hidden, excluded or denied learning because of a diagnosis.

Get InvolvedDonate Now

Every child carries God-given ability

A diagnosis is never a destiny

At Ebubechukwu Foundation we begin with what a child can do. Through free special education, speech therapy and vocational training, we help children with autism, Down syndrome, cerebral palsy and learning disabilities move from the margins to the center of family and community life.

We also recruit and train shadow teachers, and stand with parents who often carry the weight alone.
